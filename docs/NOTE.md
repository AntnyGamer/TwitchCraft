## Statement on AI Assistance

I want to be transparent about how TwitchCraft was developed.

Some parts of the application were fully generated with AI, while others were created with AI assistance. My programming experience is primarily in Lua, so C# was new to me, but I believed it was the best choice for TwitchCraft and used AI to help implement my ideas.

Every feature, mechanic, and major design decision in TwitchCraft was directed by me. I have spent hundreds of hours developing, testing, troubleshooting, refining, and improving the application with AI assistance.

I understand that some people may choose not to use software developed with AI. I hope this context makes clear both AI's role and my involvement in TwitchCraft.

Thank you for taking the time to read this statement and for considering TwitchCraft.
